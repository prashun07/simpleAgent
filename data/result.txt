The current Prime Minister of India is **Narendra Damodardas Modi**.

Here's a brief overview:

*   **Current Term:** He began his **third consecutive term** as Prime Minister on **June 9, 2024**, following the 2024 general elections.
*   **Political Party:** He is a prominent leader of the **Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)**.
*   **First Assumption of Office:** He first assumed office on **May 26, 2014**.
*   **Previous Role:** Before becoming Prime Minister, he served as the **Chief Minister of Gujarat for over 12 years** (from 2001 to 2014), making him the longest-serving Chief Minister of the state.

**Key Aspects of His Leadership and Administration:**

1.  **Economic Policies:** His governments have focused on economic reforms, infrastructure development, and initiatives like "Make in India" to boost domestic manufacturing, "Digital India" for digital transformation, and "Swachh Bharat Abhiyan" for cleanliness.
2.  **Social Welfare Programs:** Key programs include Jan Dhan Yojana (financial inclusion), Ujjwala Yojana (LPG connections for rural women), and Ayushman Bharat (national health protection scheme).
3.  **Foreign Policy:** He has actively pursued a more assertive foreign policy, engaging with various global powers and focusing on strengthening India's international standing.
4.  **National Security:** His administration has taken a strong stance on national security, particularly concerning terrorism.
5.  **Populist Appeal & Communication:** Known for his charismatic public speaking and effective use of various communication channels, including social media, to directly connect with citizens.
6.  **2024 Election:** While the BJP-led National Democratic Alliance (NDA) secured a majority, the BJP itself did not achieve a simple majority on its own (as it did in 2014 and 2019). This means his third term is leading a **coalition government**, requiring greater reliance on alliance partners.
7.  **Key Legislative Actions:** Notable decisions during his tenure include the abrogation of Article 370 in Jammu & Kashmir, the Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA), and the construction of the Ram Temple in Ayodhya.

Narendra Modi remains a highly influential and recognized political figure in India and on the global stage, having profoundly shaped India's political, economic, and social landscape over the past decade.
